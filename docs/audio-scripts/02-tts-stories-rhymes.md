**Target:** Bangladeshi Children Ages 1-5  
**AI TTS Tools:** ElevenLabs, Google Cloud TTS, Azure TTS

## 🎯 Narration Voice Guidelines

### Story Narration Settings

```
NARRATOR VOICE:
├── Tone: Warm, gentle, storytelling
├── Speed: 0.75x - 0.85x (slower for comprehension)
├── Emotion: Expressive, varies with story mood
├── Pauses: 500ms between sentences
└── Emphasis: Key vocabulary words

GOOGLE CLOUD TTS:
├── Language: bn-BD
├── Voice: bn-BD-Standard-A
├── Speaking rate: 0.8
├── Pitch: +1.0
└── Use SSML for pauses and emphasis

SSML TEMPLATE:
<speak>
  <prosody rate="slow" pitch="+1st">
    [Story text here]
    <break time="500ms"/>
    [Next sentence]
  </prosody>
</speak>
```

## 📚 গল্প (Golpo) - Short Stories

### Story 1: গরুর গল্প (Gorur Golpo) - The Cow's Story

```
=== PAGE 1 ===
একটি সবুজ মাঠে একটি গরু ছিল।
গরুর নাম ছিল মতি।

[Translation: In a green meadow lived a cow.
The cow's name was Moti.]

=== PAGE 2 ===
মতি খুব ভালো গরু ছিল।
সে প্রতিদিন সবুজ ঘাস খেত।

[Translation: Moti was a very good cow.
She ate green grass every day.]

=== PAGE 3 ===
মতি অনেক দুধ দিত।
সবাই মতির দুধ খেত।

[Translation: Moti gave lots of milk.
Everyone drank Moti's milk.]

=== PAGE 4 ===
বাচ্চারা মতিকে ভালোবাসত।
তারা মতির সাথে খেলত।

[Translation: The children loved Moti.
They played with Moti.]

=== PAGE 5 ===
মতি খুশি ছিল।
সে সবাইকে ভালোবাসত।

গল্প শেষ!

[Translation: Moti was happy.
She loved everyone.

The End!]

[Page 2 - Descriptive]
```

## 🎯 SSML Templates for TTS

### Story Narration SSML

```xml
<speak>
  <prosody rate="0.85" pitch="+1st">
    <p>
      <s>একটি সবুজ মাঠে একটি গরু ছিল।</s>
      <break time="400ms"/>
      <s>গরুর নাম ছিল <emphasis level="moderate">মতি</emphasis>।</s>
    </p>
  </prosody>
</speak>
```

### Rhyme SSML (Rhythmic)

```xml
<speak>
  <prosody rate="0.9" pitch="+2st">
    <p>
      <s>হাট্টিমা টিম টিম,</s>
      <break time="200ms"/>
      <s>তারা মাঠে পাড়ে ডিম।</s>
      <break time="300ms"/>
    </p>
  </prosody>
</speak>
```

### Lullaby SSML (Slow, Soft)

```xml
<speak>
  <prosody rate="0.7" pitch="-1st" volume="soft">
    <p>
      <s>খোকা ঘুমালো,</s>
      <break time="500ms"/>
      <s>পাড়া জুড়ালো,</s>
      <break time="400ms"/>
    </p>
  </prosody>
</speak>
```
