## 🎯 Category Style Guidelines

### Cultural Illustration Standards

```
BASE STYLE (Apply to ALL cultural prompts):
├── Art Style: "cute kawaii style", "children's book illustration"
├── Character: "friendly characters", "big expressive eyes"
├── Rendering: "flat design", "soft shadows", "clean outlines"
├── Colors: "vibrant Bengali colors", "warm festive tones"
├── Background: "transparent background", "white background for extraction"
├── Quality: "high quality", "vector art", "4K", "clean edges"
└── Mood: "celebratory", "warm", "family-friendly", "joyful"

CULTURAL AUTHENTICITY:
├── Traditional Bengali clothing (শাড়ি, পাঞ্জাবি, লুঙ্গি)
├── Bengali patterns (নকশা, আল্পনা)
├── Local architecture elements
├── Authentic festival decorations
├── Regional flora and fauna
└── Respectful cultural representation

NEGATIVE PROMPTS (Add to all):
"--no realistic, photorealistic, complex details, 
dark colors, scary elements, religious symbols, 
3D render, adult themes, culturally insensitive"
```
