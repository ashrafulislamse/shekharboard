## 🎯 Category Style Guidelines

### Fruit Illustration Standards

```
BASE STYLE (Apply to ALL fruit prompts):
├── Art Style: "cute kawaii style", "children's book illustration"
├── Character: "happy face on fruit", "big round eyes", "small smile"
├── Rendering: "flat design", "soft shadows", "clean outlines"
├── Colors: "vibrant but soft", "natural fruit colors", "pastel accents"
├── Background: "transparent background", "white background for extraction"
├── Quality: "high quality", "vector art", "4K", "clean edges"
└── Mood: "fresh", "yummy", "friendly", "appetizing"

CONSISTENCY RULES:
├── All fruits have cute kawaii faces (optional for some)
├── Same eye style (big, round, sparkly with highlight)
├── Similar level of simplification
├── Consistent shadow direction (bottom-right)
├── Transparent/white background for app integration
├── Show recognizable fruit shape clearly
└── Leaf/stem details where appropriate

NEGATIVE PROMPTS (Add to all):
"--no realistic, photorealistic, detailed texture, 
complex shading, dark colors, rotting, bruised, 
3D render, adult style, scary face"
```
