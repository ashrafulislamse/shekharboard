## 🎯 Category Style Guidelines

### Vehicle Illustration Standards

```
BASE STYLE (Apply to ALL vehicle prompts):
├── Art Style: "cute kawaii style", "children's book illustration"
├── Character: "friendly vehicle with face", "headlights as eyes"
├── Rendering: "flat design", "soft shadows", "clean outlines"
├── Colors: "vibrant colors", "warm tones", "soft saturation"
├── Background: "transparent background", "white background for extraction"
├── Quality: "high quality", "vector art", "4K", "clean edges"
└── Mood: "friendly", "fun", "safe", "approachable"

CONSISTENCY RULES:
├── Headlights become friendly eyes (or add face on windshield)
├── Grille becomes smile
├── Same eye style across all vehicles
├── Simplified details (no complex mechanics)
├── Consistent shadow direction (bottom-right)
├── Transparent/white background for app integration
├── Show vehicle from 3/4 angle for recognition
└── Bright cheerful colors (red, blue, green, yellow)

NEGATIVE PROMPTS (Add to all):
"--no realistic, photorealistic, detailed mechanics, 
complex shading, dark colors, accidents, damaged, 
3D render, adult style, scary, military vehicles"
```
